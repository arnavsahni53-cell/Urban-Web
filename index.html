The challenge

Every city has what it needs.
None of it connects.

Cities invest in buses, metros, parking systems, and workforce programmes. The demand is there. The intent is real.

And yet: a hospital can't offer a patient transport at the moment they book an appointment, because connecting to a transit operator takes a year of custom work. An employer wants to reward green commuting but can only estimate it from surveys. Every one of these problems has the same root — not missing infrastructure, but missing conditions for that infrastructure to connect.

City mobility today
Custom build for every pair. Built once, for one use. Full cost, every time. The city never sees the whole picture.

Urban Web

The network works because the standard is open — not because anyone controls it. Urban Web brings that shift to cities.

Six stories

Every city has different priorities.
Every city has the same underlying opportunity.

Story 01

When the daily commute becomes a city asset.

Cities have modal shift targets. Most of the lever sits in the daily commute. What if every employer in your city could run a verified, auditable sustainable commute programme across every transit operator — without a single bilateral agreement? And what if every trip became proof the city could use?

Follow the journey
1 / 3
Employers Set a commute policy once. Every transit operator in the city enforces it automatically. No bilateral agreements.
Employees Credits arrive the moment the trip completes. No form, no claim, no admin overhead.
Carbon reporting Verified trip data flows to registries automatically. Proof, not estimates. Auditable by any framework.
Cities Track modal shift across all employers and operators. A real evidence base for transport investment.
Story 02

When mobility finds people instead of waiting for them.

The patient confirming an appointment, the employee seeing a shift, the event-goer buying a ticket — each left to find their own way. What if mobility was already present at the moment the journey was decided, embedded in the platforms people already use?

See how it works
1 / 4
Hospitals Offer transport at the moment of appointment confirmation. Fewer missed appointments. No integration work.
Event organisers Bundle the transit leg with every ticket sold. Reduce venue congestion, measurably.
Transit operators Receive demand from every connected service in the city — without negotiating each one separately.
Cities Stop building platforms. Every hospital, hotel, and retailer becomes a mobility distribution channel.
Story 03

When every parking space becomes a city resource.

Every city has spaces sitting idle and demand that cannot find them. The supply is there. The connections are not. What if every space — public, private, basement, residential — was visible, bookable, and connected to the city around it?

Cities See all supply and demand in real time. Use dynamic pricing to reduce congestion without owning the spaces.

Story 04

When driving becomes aspirational.

Cities are committing to electric fleets — but the transition depends on whether the workforce is ready. What if a city could see its skills gaps before they become a crisis? What if driving became a career where skill is recognised, rewarded, and worth investing in?

A credential that travels
1 / 4
Drivers Every qualification and employment record is portable. Skill commands recognition and better pay across every employer.
Operators Verified candidates discoverable immediately. No certificates to chase. Hire with confidence the same day.
Cities Workforce readiness visible in aggregate. EV skills gaps identified before they become crises.
Training institutes Graduates are discoverable by operators searching for their certification from the moment the course completes.
Story 05

When idle city capacity becomes a public resource.

Every city has a fleet of school buses empty by day, corporate coaches idle on weekends, tourist vehicles parked for months. What if that latent capacity — already paid for — could become part of the city's transit offer, available to any operator, any platform, any commuter?

Capacity without procurement
1 / 4
Fleet owners Publish available windows once. Any operator or platform can find and book them. Revenue with no sales effort.
Cities Expand transit capacity without procurement. Use what already exists before buying anything new.
Commuters Access to more routes, more frequently, at lower cost — without waiting for new infrastructure.
Operators Fill demand spikes using verified capacity from verified providers. No custom contracts required.
Story 06

When the city's energy and mobility become one system.

Every electric vehicle is a battery on wheels. Every office rooftop has solar. Every parked bus fleet is a grid asset waiting to be used. What if every energy asset in the city could find every other — and transact automatically?

Energy and mobility connected
1 / 4
Drivers Every charge point discoverable, bookable, and settled automatically — regardless of operator or network.
Buildings Solar and battery storage becomes revenue. Export to the grid at peak demand, automatically.
Transit operators Electric fleets stabilise the grid and generate income while parked, via vehicle-to-grid participation.
Cities Grid investment guided by real usage data. Infrastructure built where it's actually needed — not estimated.
